## How to write a new article

Copy this file, rename it to your slug, and change the frontmatter at the top.
Only five fields need thought:

- **title** — the symptom in the owner's words, not the category name
- **description** — 150–160 characters. This is the meta description *and* the
  text Facebook shows on the card
- **category** — one of: Revenue Leaks, AI Workforce, Operations Teardowns,
  Building Strata Core
- **publishedAt** — `YYYY-MM-DD`
- **draft** — `true` keeps it out of the site entirely

Everything below the second `---` is the article, written in Markdown.

## The structure that works

1. The situation — a specific operational moment the reader recognises
2. What actually breaks — the mechanism, not adjectives
3. Why it keeps happening — the structural cause
4. What to do about it — concrete, and useful even without Strata
5. Where Strata fits — short and honest

## Two rules that are not style preferences

Strata Core is **in development**. Never describe it as shipped, available, or
something a customer can log into today.

Never quote a client result that has not been verified, and never name a client
without written permission.
